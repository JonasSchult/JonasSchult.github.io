I am a Senior AI Research Scientist at Meta in London, taking generative image and video models from research into production. Before joining Meta, I did my PhD research on 3D Scene Understanding at RWTH Aachen University with Prof. Dr. Bastian Leibe. I received my B.Sc. and my M.Sc. (with distinction) in Computer Science from RWTH Aachen University, where I worked as a student research assistant under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Francis Engelmann.

News

  • Feb 2026: TUNA (Highlight) and HiStream were accepted to CVPR 2026. Congrats to Weiming Ren, Zhiheng Liu and Haonan Qiu on their internship projects!
  • Sep 2024: The third iteration of our workshop OpenSUN3D on Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Understanding was held in conjunction with ECCV 2024 in Milan.
  • Aug 2024: I joined Meta in London as an AI Research Scientist, working on generative image and video models.
  • Feb 2024: ControlRoom3D was accepted to CVPR 2024.
  • Jan 2024: AGILE3D was accepted at ICLR and Mask4Former was accepted at ICRA. Congrats to both Yuanwen Yue and Kadir Yilmaz on their successful master theses.
Older news (2022–2023)
  • Dec 2023: The second iteration of our workshop OpenSun3D on Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Understanding will be held in conjunction with CVPR in Seattle.
  • Aug 2023: Point2Vec was accepted at GCPR 2023.
  • Jul 2023: Human3D was accepted at ICCV 2023. This was a great collaboration with Ayça Takmaz, Irem Kaftan, Mertcan Akçay, Bastian Leibe, Robert Sumner, Francis Engelmann and Siyu Tang!
  • Jun-Nov 2023: I will intern at Meta Gen AI, looking forward to working with Sam Tsai!
  • Mar 2023: Our workshop OpenSun3D on Open-Vocabulary 3D Scene Understanding will be held in conjunction with ICCV'23 in Paris.
  • Jan 2023: Mask3D was accepted at ICRA 2023.
  • Oct 2022: I gave a talk about our recent work Mask3D at the Urban3D Workshop at ECCV 2022. You can find the recording here.
  • Sep 2022: Our Mask3D method ranks 2nd on the Instance Segmentation Challenge hosted by the Urban3D Workshop at ECCV 2022.
  • Sep 2022: Mask3D achieves SOTA on the challenging ScanNet, ScanNet200, S3DIS and STPLS3D dataset.
  • Jun 2022: Mix3D won first place on the ScanNet 3D Semantic Segmentation challenge. This was a great team effort with Alexey Nekrasov, Francis Engelmann, Or Litany and Bastian Leibe!
